Apps

Card scanner and deck builder

In development · iPhone

Our first app is for Magic: The Gathering players. Point your camera at a card to identify the exact printing and see its current market price. Keep your whole collection on your phone, and build decks from the cards you already own.

  • ScanIdentify a card and its printing in about a second, even offline.
  • PriceMarket value for every card and your whole collection, updated daily.
  • BuildDecks from your own library, shown as boxes on a shelf.

Planned for release on the App Store in 2027.
