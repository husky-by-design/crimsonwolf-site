Privacy policy

This policy covers the crimsonwolf.llc website and the PackTutor™ app for iPhone, both run by Crimson Wolf LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company.

The short version

  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your camera stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send a scan report.
  • AI features send your cards and decks to a third-party AI service only after you agree on a consent screen.
  • Friends see your name, level and achievements, and only the decks you choose to share. Never your collection.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app (Profile → Delete account), and that removes everything stored with it.

What this website collects

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no forms or accounts. We don't collect personal information through it.

Like any website, our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We keep that correspondence only as long as we need it to help you, and we never sell or share it.

The PackTutor app

PackTutor is a collection app for Magic: The Gathering cards. Here's what it collects and why. Scanning, card details and prices work without an account.

Your account

To create an account, you sign in with Apple or with an email address and a passphrase. We store your email address. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only get an Apple private relay address, not your real one. Passphrases are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form; we can't read them.

Your profile

You can choose a display name and an avatar. Both are optional. They're stored with Supabase. The other player sees them when you trade, and your friends see them on your profile.

Your collection, decks and progress

When you're signed in, the following sync with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States (US East), so they're on all your devices. Only you can see them, except the decks you share with your friends (see Friends).

  • Your collection: cards, quantities, condition, finish, pinned cards, and how each card was added (for example, scanned, or imported along with the name of the app it came from).
  • Your decks: their cards, format, deck box style, your deck notes (your game journal), who can see each deck (only you, or your friends), whether it's planned or built, and, for a deck you cloned, the display name of the friend it came from.
  • Your want list.
  • Your rewards progress, such as achievements and set milestones, and your total XP.

Imported files

When you import a CSV file or a deck list from another app, the file is read on your phone. Only the cards that end up in your collection sync. Imported deck lists stay on your phone.

Trades

When you trade cards with another player, we store the two accounts, the cards each of you offers, and your confirmations with Supabase. Only the two of you can see a trade. The other player sees only your display name, your avatar and the cards in the trade, not the rest of your collection or your email.

Trade codes work for 24 hours. To stop codes being guessed, if you type a code that matches no trade, we log that attempt against your account and keep it for 7 days.

Friends

Friends are optional. You get a friend code, and you add a friend by entering theirs, or from a deck they've made public. You become friends only when the other player accepts. There's no public list of players: nobody can find you unless they have your code or you've made a deck public, and you can get a new code at any time.

When you're friends, each of you can see the other's:

  • Display name and avatar.
  • XP, level and rank.
  • Achievements, and the dates they were earned.
  • Decks set to Friends or Everyone: their name, description, format, deck box style and cards.

You can also send a deck or your want list to a friend. Only that friend can then see it: a private deck you send stays hidden from everyone else, and your want list shows the cards you want, never what you own. We store who you sent it to, so they can keep opening it while you're friends.

Friends never see your collection, your deck notes, your email address, or decks and a want list you haven't shared with them. Every deck starts as visible only to you, and you can change who sees it at any time.

When you clone a friend's deck, the copy is yours: it's saved with your decks, along with the friend's display name, and later changes to their deck don't change it.

We store your friend code, your friends and friend requests, and anyone you block, with Supabase. To stop codes being guessed, if you type a friend code that matches nobody, we log that attempt against your account and keep it for 7 days.

You can remove a friend or block a player at any time. Blocking ends the friendship and stops that player sending you requests. You can also report a player or one of their shared decks. A report stores your account, the account and deck you reported, and what you wrote. Reports are kept privately, seen only by us, and never shown to the player you reported.

Public decks

You can set a deck to Everyone. Anyone using PackTutor can then find it in Discover → Popular decks and see its name, description, format, deck box style and cards, along with your display name, avatar and level. They can clone it, report it, or send you a friend request from it. Public decks never show your collection, your game notes, your email address or your other decks.

When someone clones a public deck, we store that they did, once per player, to count how often it's been cloned. The count is shown with the deck; who cloned it isn't.

A public deck reported by three different players is hidden until we review it. You can set a deck back to Friends or Only me at any time, and it leaves Popular decks straight away.

Notifications

The app keeps a list of your notifications, behind the bell at the top of each screen: friend requests and accepted requests, updates on trades you're in (when the other player joins, confirms, finishes or cancels), and decks a friend shares with you. Each entry stores who it's from, what it's about, when it happened and whether you've read it. Only you can see your list. It's stored with Supabase, linked to your account, and kept until you delete your account.

If you allow notifications, the app also tells us your phone's push token, a code from Apple that lets us send a notification to that phone. We store it with Supabase, linked to your account, and use it only to send you the entries above as notifications, except shared decks, which stay in the list. A notification shows the other player's display name and, for a new friend, how many decks they share with friends. It's delivered by Apple's push notification service. Signing out removes the token, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Camera and scanning

When you scan a card, the camera frames are processed on your phone to read the card. Camera frames and photos are never uploaded, unless you choose to send a scan report.

Scan reports

If the scanner gets a card wrong, you can choose to send us a scan report. A report includes a photo of the card, the text the scanner recognized, any note you add, the app version and your iPhone model. Reports are stored privately with Supabase, are seen only by us, and are used only to improve scanning. We never send a report without you choosing to.

When a new report comes in, it's emailed to our team through Resend, an email delivery service, with your display name and account email so we can reply. We may also get a short notification on our phones through ntfy.sh that contains only the card name and printing: no notes, emails or account details.

Feedback

When you're signed in, Profile → Send feedback sends us your message, a screenshot if you pick one, the app version, your iOS version and your iPhone model. Feedback is stored privately with Supabase and emailed to our team through Resend, with your account email as the reply address so we can answer you. You can send a few messages an hour.

When you're signed out, Send feedback opens your own mail app instead, and the email works like any other email to us.

AI features

PackTutor Pro includes AI deck building, deck tune-ups and card explanations. They're also available to beta testers. Before your first AI request, the app shows a consent screen. If you don't agree, nothing is sent and the rest of the app works as before.

When you use a cloud AI feature, the app sends the following through our server to a third-party AI service, which uses it to produce the answer:

  • The cards you own that are legal for the deck (card names and details).
  • The deck being tuned, with its name and description.
  • Your deck notes, when you ask for a tune-up or a summary.

We keep a count of AI requests for each account to enforce monthly limits. We don't keep the content of your requests.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, some small AI features, like explaining a card, run on your phone with Apple's on-device model. Nothing leaves your phone for those.

AI can make mistakes, so check its suggestions.

Card images and prices

Card details, images and prices load from Scryfall. When the app loads them, Scryfall receives the request from your phone, including your IP address, as any website would. We don't send Scryfall your account or collection.

Other services your phone contacts

  • Card data and daily price updates download from our storage at Supabase.
  • News headlines load from our server. Opening a headline opens the publisher's own website.
  • "Read the full rules" loads Wizards of the Coast's rules PDF from their website. We don't save the PDF.
  • Buy links open TCGplayer or Cardmarket.

Each of these sees your phone's IP address, as any website would, but not your account or your collection.

Purchases

Any purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details; Apple tells us only whether you have an active purchase.

Beta testing

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you submit with us, under Apple's own privacy policy. We use them only to fix and improve the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, or share it for advertising.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app doesn't ask for permission to.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase: sign-in, your synced data, friends, trades, reports, scan reports and feedback (United States).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, purchases, TestFlight, push notifications, and on-device AI.
  • Scryfall: card details, images and prices.
  • A third-party AI service: only for cloud AI features, and only after you agree.
  • Resend: emails of scan reports and feedback to our team.
  • ntfy.sh: short notifications to our team, with no personal details.
  • Wizards of the Coast: only when you open the rules PDF.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete it.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account). That permanently removes your account and everything in it: your collection, decks, want list, profile, rewards, friends and friend requests, blocks, reports you sent, push tokens, notifications, decks and want lists you sent to friends, clone counts from your account, trades, AI usage counts, feedback and its screenshots, and scan reports and their photos. Trades you were part of are removed for the other player too, and you disappear from your friends' lists. Decks your friends cloned from you stay with them as their own copies, with your display name as where they came from.

Scan reports and feedback that were already emailed to our team inbox can be deleted on request. You can also ask us to delete your data, or for a copy of what we hold, by emailing contact@crimsonwolf.llc from the address on your account.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
